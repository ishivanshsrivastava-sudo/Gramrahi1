TECH FOR SOCIAL GOOD

The Problem We Are Solving

In many villages and underserved areas of India, people reach a hospital with no health history, no record of past treatment, and no easy way to prove which scheme they are covered by. In an emergency, doctors start from zero and families lose precious time. Paper records and vaccination cards get lost, damaged or left at home.

👥 Who it helps

Rural and underserved citizens, parents of young children, senior citizens, and patients who cannot speak for themselves in an emergency.

💡 How Gramrahi helps

One Patient ID, a photo to identify the patient, and photos of prescriptions and vaccination cards stored safely, so any hospital can see what it needs to start treatment.

🎯 UN SDG alignment

SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities): healthcare access that does not depend on paperwork, language or location.

☁️ Powered by Cloudinary. Patient photos, prescriptions and vaccination-card photos are uploaded with the Cloudinary Upload Widget (camera or gallery). They are then delivered auto-cropped (face-aware), auto-compressed and in the best format for each phone, so records load fast even on slow rural networks.

Child Healthcare

👶 Child Healthcare Centre

One place for your children's basic health records and care reminders.

1. Parent and child profiles

Add each child under this browser's local demo profile. Do not use this prototype to store sensitive medical records on a shared device.

2. Vaccination tracker

Select a child to view a starter checklist. This is not a complete or personalized vaccine schedule: confirm dose names and due dates with your child's vaccination card or ANM/ASHA/health centre.

SMS/WhatsApp reminders are not sent by this front-end demo. They require a secure backend and a messaging provider.

3. Growth and nutrition check

Growth interpretation in children requires age- and sex-specific standards and accurate measurements. This prototype saves measurements and prompts appropriate follow-up; it does not diagnose malnutrition.

4. Child symptom guide

This guide is not a diagnosis. If the child seems seriously unwell, do not wait for this checker.

5. Child-specific emergency

Capture the child's age and symptoms, then continue to the existing ambulance page.

For an immediate emergency in India, call 112 or your local emergency service now. This demo does not dispatch an ambulance automatically.

6. Child and maternal scheme finder

These are starting points to discuss with an ASHA/ANM, Anganwadi worker or government health centre. Availability and eligibility depend on current official rules and location.

Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)

Ask a public health facility about eligible free services for pregnant women and sick newborns/infants under the programme.

Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK)

Ask about child health screening and referral services for eligible children.

Anganwadi / ICDS services

Ask your local Anganwadi about nutrition support, growth monitoring and services available in your area.

Ayushman Bharat / PM-JAY

Check current state-specific eligibility and covered services through official channels; this card does not confirm eligibility.
